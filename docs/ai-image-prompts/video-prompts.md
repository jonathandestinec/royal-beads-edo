# AI video prompts (Higgsfield, image-to-video)

Each clip starts from one of our AI concept images so the faces, beads and colours match the site. Make each clip 5 to 8 seconds, 16:9 if the model allows (it is used full-screen on desktop), no text, no music. Download the MP4 and drop it in the Drive folder; it will be graded, trimmed to a seamless loop and labelled as a concept clip.

## 1. Opening (chapter 01, behind the scribble intro)

Start image: `1-hero-coral-necklace-portrait.jpg`

> Slow cinematic push-in on a young Edo Nigerian woman in three-quarter profile, eyes closed, chin lifted, wearing a chunky necklace of polished red-orange coral beads and a coral earring. She breathes in gently and opens her eyes slowly, a faint smile. Warm golden-hour light drifts across her skin and the coral beads catch the light. Plain warm brown wall, soft dust in the air. Shallow depth of field, 35mm film look, fine grain, subtle handheld drift, calm and dignified. No text.
