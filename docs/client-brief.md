# Client brief: Royal Beads Edo

Interview held Mon 23 Nov with the client (a teammate playing the owner).

## The business
Coral beads and traditional attire seller in the Oba Market area of Benin City, Edo State.

## The problem
Last Saturday a customer received a bead set that didn't match the style they expected from a static photo. They wished they could have tried it on first to see it with their attire.

## Customers
- About 25 inquiries a week, many from the UK and US.
- Most requested: wedding sets for bride and groom.

## Top customer questions
1. Is it real coral? (Buyers often worry about fake coral.)
2. How long does a custom set take? (2 to 3 weeks.)
3. Do you ship abroad?

## The never rule
**Never confirm a custom-set ready date.** Only the workshop can confirm ready dates. The website, chatbot and every feature must respect this.

## Needs a human
Custom set requests for an event less than 3 weeks away.

## What the client wants on the site
- Bead sets shown by occasion
- The cultural and traditional meaning of the beads
- Clear steps for how custom orders work
- A direct order (Enquire) button
