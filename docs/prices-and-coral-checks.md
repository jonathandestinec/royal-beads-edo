## How to tell real coral (non-destructive checks only)

1. **Look closely.** Natural coral shows fine growth lines, tiny pits and slight colour changes from bead to bead; plastic and glass look perfectly even.
2. **Feel the weight.** Coral is dense, so it feels cool and heavier than plastic beads of the same size ([GemsRoot](https://www.gemsroot.com/blog/how-to-identify-original-red-coral-stone)).
3. **Check the holes.** Colour that is darker inside drill holes or cracks suggests dye ([The Bead Traders](https://www.thebeadtraders.com/blogs/news/natural-dyed-coral-beads-how-to-spot-the-difference)).
4. **Skip home tests.** Acid, flame and scratch tests damage real coral; for valuable pieces, a gemologist's check is the reliable option ([Gem Society](https://www.gemsociety.org/article/destructive-tests-acid-testing/), [Mindat](https://www.mindat.org/mesg-293689.html)).

The "milk test" and "turmeric test" that circulate online are not reliable, so the site and chatbot do not recommend them.
